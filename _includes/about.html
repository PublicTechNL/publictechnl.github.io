Over PUBLIC TECH NL

In 2015 switchte Johan Groenen van startups naar overheid. Als Code Fellow bij de gemeente Amsterdam stond hij aan de wieg van Datalab Amsterdam en een groot aantal innovatieve toepassingen die daar ontstonden.

Hij was oprichter van onder andere Code for NL (community) en Tiltshift (consultancy).

Sinds 2025 werken we vanuit PUBLIC TECH NL pro-actief aan innovatieve oplossingsconcepten - al dan niet gesubsidieerd.

Sinds 2015 is PUBLIC TECH NL een collaborative innovation lab dedicated to improving public services through technology, design, and policy innovation. We believe that the best solutions emerge when diverse perspectives come together to solve complex problems.

Our team brings together expertise from government, technology, design, and academia to create an interdisciplinary approach to public sector challenges. We work as partners with government agencies, municipalities, and civic organizations to develop solutions that are not just innovative but also practical and implementable.

PUBLIC TECH NL is committed to open innovation and sharing knowledge across the public sector. Our work is guided by principles of transparency, citizen-centricity, and sustainable impact.
