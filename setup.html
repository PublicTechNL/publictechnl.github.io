About PUBLIC TECH NL

Founded in 2023, PUBLIC TECH NL is a collaborative innovation lab dedicated to improving public services through technology, design, and policy innovation. We believe that the best solutions emerge when diverse perspectives come together to solve complex problems.

Our team brings together expertise from government, technology, design, and academia to create an interdisciplinary approach to public sector challenges. We work as partners with government agencies, municipalities, and civic organizations to develop solutions that are not just innovative but also practical and implementable.

PUBLIC TECH NL is committed to open innovation and sharing knowledge across the public sector. Our work is guided by principles of transparency, citizen-centricity, and sustainable impact.
